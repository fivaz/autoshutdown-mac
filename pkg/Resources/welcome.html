AutoShutdown

This installs a small menu bar app that shuts this Mac down at the same time every evening.

  • Warnings at 15 minutes, 5 minutes, 1 minute and 30 seconds before shutdown.
  • One extension of 15 minutes per day, and no more. The time left can only shrink.
  • An optional commitment lock freezes the settings until a date and hour you choose.

The app is installed in Applications and starts automatically at every login. Turning the Mac back on after a shutdown is expected and nothing prevents it.

Removing it later: run uninstall.sh from the project, or delete the app and /Library/LaunchAgents/com.fivaz.autoshutdown.plist.
