Installed and running

  1. Look for the power icon in the menu bar. It shows the time left until shutdown.
  2. Open Settings… from that menu to set your shutdown time. The default is 23:00.
  3. The first time the app prepares a shutdown, macOS asks whether AutoShutdown may control System Events. Allow it, otherwise the shutdown cannot happen.

If you missed that prompt, the menu shows a warning and takes you to the right pane of System Settings.
